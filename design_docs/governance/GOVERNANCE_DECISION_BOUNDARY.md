# Governance Decision Boundary

## Purpose

This document separates governance actions from research actions for the approved portfolio:

- `Core BTC holding`
- `Sleeve #1 = ConstAddOn[1.00x]`
- `Sleeve #2 = RangeRotation`
- hard cap `3.0x`

## Governance Domain

Governance is allowed to decide:

- whether current deployment posture remains full, partial, or temporarily reduced
- whether cap-usage comfort has changed
- whether alert thresholds require higher review intensity
- whether a de-risk intervention is warranted
- whether re-engagement clearance has been earned
- whether `2.5x` fallback posture should be used due to governance tightening

Governance is also allowed to define:

- review cadence
- escalation protocol
- reporting standards
- incident handling

## Research Domain

Research retains authority over:

- mother strategy
- execution tuples
- Sleeve #1 logic
- Sleeve #2 logic
- archived / frozen branch status
- any future sleeve discovery
- any strategy or parameter changes

## Non-Negotiable Frozen Items

The following remain frozen and off-limits during governance intervention:

- old standalone `Risk-Off` promotion line
- `compression_breakout -> exhaustion / compound repair`
- single-sleeve dynamic timing research
- regime cap
- breakout grading / cap / downgrade micro-research
- rejected second-sleeve and third-sleeve families

## Governance May Not

Governance may not:

- reopen frozen `Risk-Off`
- rewrite sleeve triggers
- add re-entry indicators
- add market-state signal logic
- reinterpret alerts as automated trade rules
- relabel frozen research as operational necessity

## Governance May Reduce Only Posture

What governance may reduce:

- deployment posture
- allowed sleeve stacking posture
- live cap usage tolerance

What governance may not reduce or rewrite:

- the research baseline itself
- the approved signal ecology
- the strategy identity of the portfolio

## Escalation Rule

If governance concludes that practical operation requires:

- new timing rules
- new regime logic
- new sleeve behavior
- new exposure automation beyond posture control

then the issue must be handed back to research as a new explicitly authorized stage.

## Operational Test For Boundary Discipline

A governance rule is valid only if it can be described as:

- review-driven
- rare
- explicit
- posture-level

A governance rule becomes invalid if it behaves like:

- frequent signal logic
- automated market timing
- hidden alpha discovery
- informal strategy mutation

## Final Boundary Statement

Governance controls deployment posture.
Research controls strategy logic.

If posture control starts to imitate signal logic, the framework has crossed the boundary and must stop.
